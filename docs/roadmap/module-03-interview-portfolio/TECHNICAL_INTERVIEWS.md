# Technical interviews

Allow 5 minutes for the questions and 10 minutes for the drill. Work with local files, paper SQL, or scratch data. Use the answer key after committing to an approach. B, I and A indicate beginner, intermediate and advanced.

| Topic and repository evidence | Questions | Practical drill | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SQL and BigQuery** · [Order fact](../../../analytics/models/marts/fct_orders.sql) | **B** What is the fact grain? **I** How would you report monthly delivered payment totals and missing-payment counts? **A** How do you prove a rewrite avoids fan-out and bound its cost? | Trace one order with two payments and three items through a direct join and a pre-aggregated join. State the row and sum invariants. | Joining two one-to-many inputs directly or treating missing payment as zero without disclosure. |
| **Python and Pandas** · [Experiment validation](../../../experimentation/analyze_experiment.py) | **B** What does a binary outcome mean? **I** How do you validate an ID before merging? **A** How would you avoid silent loss when an extract contains duplicate assignments and null outcomes? | Sketch a Pandas validation step for a clean group and a malformed group. Return variant size and purchase counts only after validation. | Using non-null outcome count as the assigned denominator. |
| **dbt and dimensional modeling** · [Model documentation](../../../analytics/README.md) | **B** What do source and ref represent? **I** When should a model stay order-grain? **A** How would you detect a broken relationship or a target that accidentally enables another branch? | Draw staging, aggregation and fact dependencies. Propose a grain test, a reconciliation test and an offline target check. | Assuming a passing uniqueness test proves the amounts reconcile. |
| **Data pipelines and Airflow** · [Local DAG guide](../../../orchestration/airflow/README.md) | **B** What does the DAG schedule? **I** Why does the FX task use logical date? **A** What happens after a partial load and retry or concurrent backfill? | An FX task wrote raw JSON and failed before warehouse completion. Describe evidence to inspect before a replay and when to release downstream tasks. | Claiming the DAG ingests Olist CSVs or guarantees exactly-once writes. |
| **Product analytics and GA4** · [Historical GA4 methods](../../../analytics/models/ga4/README.md) | **B** Why count sessions? **I** Why might a purchase event fail to qualify for the sequential funnel? **A** How do you explain differences between attribution proxy, native GA4 and app-session reporting? | Draw a session with add-to-cart before view, followed by checkout and purchase. State the qualifying historical stage counts. Then add a second cart after the view and explain the difference. | Reporting event totals as ordered session conversions. |
| **Power BI and DAX** · [Cohort report](../../powerbi/customer-retention-page.md) | **B** What is filter context? **I** How does a weighted cohort card differ from a mean of visible percentages? **A** How do you distinguish a DAX issue from a model, slicer or PBIR formatting issue? | A card says 0.0K while a table shows 37. List checks in order before changing the measure. Use the [dashboard binding test](../../../tests/test_ga4_commerce_dashboard.py) as local supporting evidence. | Changing DAX before checking display units, precision and filters. |
| **A/B and statistics** · [Synthetic case study](../../../experimentation/CASE_STUDY.md) | **B** What is the experimental unit? **I** Why can an effect be significant and still fail a business threshold? **A** What invalidates a fixed-horizon inference if the decision rule changes mid-test? | Give a 90-second go/no-go response using the documented effect, interval, break-even and guardrails. Defend it under a request to stop early. | Treating significance as launch approval or synthetic results as real impact. |
| **Cohort retention and funnels** · [Retention mart](../../../analytics/models/marts/customer_cohort_retention.sql) · [GA4 stages](../../../analytics/models/ga4/README.md) | **B** What identifies a repeat buyer? **I** Why does a future cell stay blank? **A** What changes when weighting cohorts or moving from all-session to stage-entry conversion? | Review a chart comparing June M2 and August M2 at an August cutoff. Specify a corrected comparison and denominator. | Turning unobserved cells into zero or averaging unequal cohort percentages. |
| **Forecasting and business decisions** · [Forecast evaluation](../../forecasting/README.md) | **B** Explain MAE in BRL. **I** When would RMSE alter the choice? **A** How can a chronological split still leak future information? | Defend one planning baseline and propose a prospective evaluation that respects status maturity and forecast origin. | Randomly splitting weeks or claiming the point forecast is a revenue commitment. |
| **Data quality, CI/CD and debugging** · [CI workflow](../../../.github/workflows/ci.yml) | **B** What does a parse check validate? **I** Which test detects a fan-out and which detects missing keys? **A** What evidence would you need to claim a release is safe? | CI passes but a dashboard total doubles. Trace from visual filters to semantic grain, model outputs and joins. Identify the first divergent count or sum. | Treating a green offline workflow as warehouse validation or deployment. |
